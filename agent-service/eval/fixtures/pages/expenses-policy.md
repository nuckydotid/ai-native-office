# Expenses policy

You can spend up to USD 150 per purchase without asking anyone. Above that amount the finance lead approves in writing before you buy.

## Receipts

Submit receipts within 21 days of the purchase. Receipts older than that are not reimbursed.

## Travel

Hotels are reimbursed up to USD 160 per night.
