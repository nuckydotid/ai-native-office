# On-call and incident runbook

## Severity levels

SEV1 means customers cannot use the app at all. SEV2 means a major feature is broken for many customers.

## Response times

The on-call engineer acknowledges a SEV1 page within 10 minutes. The status page is updated within 20 minutes of declaring a SEV1 or SEV2.

## Contact details

Phone numbers live in the paging tool. They are deliberately not written on this page.
