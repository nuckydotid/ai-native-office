# Remote work and working hours

Everyone at the fixture company works remotely. Core hours are 10:00 to 14:00 Singapore time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours people choose their own schedule.

## Working from another country

You may work from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year. Tell the people lead two weeks ahead so the tax and insurance questions can be checked.

## Equipment

The company pays for a home workspace budget of USD 400 once, at the start of employment.
