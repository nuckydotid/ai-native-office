# Weekly planning, 6 October 2026

Attendees: Ines Carvalho, Owen Whitaker, Kai Nakamura, Zara Haddad, Leo Brandt. Notes by Owen.

## Agenda

1. Where we are on offline sync
2. What goes in the Q4 roadmap
3. Pilot customers

## Notes

**Offline sync.** Kai has the local database and the operation queue working on his own phone. The next piece is the server side: the operation log and batch upload. Kai expects the main work to take about three weeks.

**Roadmap.** Owen showed the first draft of the Q4 roadmap. Barcode scanning for equipment tags was added after two customers asked for it. Dark mode was on the draft. Zara asked what would be dropped to make room, and the group agreed that dark mode should go.

**Dark mode.** It was postponed because of what the customers said. We asked the three pilot candidates (Harbourline Logistics, Ashgrove Facilities and Brightwater Services) what they most wanted from the next three months. All three said offline work first. None of them mentioned dark mode. Leo pointed out that the design work for dark mode is also larger than it looks, because every inspection screen would need checking. We will look at it again in Q1 2027.

**Pilot customers.** Harbourline Logistics is the most ready of the three. Owen will send them a short list of what we need from them before a start date can be fixed.

## Decisions

- Dark mode moves out of Q4 and is revisited in Q1 2027.
- Barcode scanning is added to Q4 and Zara owns it.
- Harbourline Logistics is the first pilot customer.
