# Remote work and working hours

Owner: Ines Carvalho (founder). Last reviewed on 2 September 2026.

Northwind is a remote-first team of five. We work from different cities, so we keep one reference clock: **Singapore time (UTC+8)**. Times on this page and in the rest of the handbook are Singapore time unless they say otherwise.

## Core hours

Core hours are **10:00 to 14:00 Singapore time**, Monday to Friday. During core hours everyone is online in the studio, answers the lobby within a few minutes and can join a call without notice. The daily standup is at 10:15 in the lobby.

Outside core hours we work asynchronously. Nobody is expected to answer a message in the evening, and a message sent late is not a request to reply late.

## Meeting-free time

Wednesday afternoons, from 14:00, have no meetings. Use them for focused work. Customer calls are the only exception, and Owen approves those in advance.

## Working from another country

You may work from outside your home country for up to **30 days per calendar year** without asking for permission. Tell Owen at least two weeks before you travel and make sure you can still cover core hours, or agree a different pattern with him first. Longer stays need Ines's approval, because tax and employment rules differ by country.

## Equipment and workspace

Northwind provides a laptop and a phone for testing. A one-off budget for a home workspace is described in the expenses policy. Keep work devices up to date and follow the security basics page.

## Public holidays

Follow the public holiday calendar of the country you live in. Add your holidays to the shared calendar at least a week ahead so release planning can allow for them.

## When you cannot work

If you cannot work, tell the lobby as early as you can. For production problems outside core hours, the on-call engineer follows the incident runbook.
