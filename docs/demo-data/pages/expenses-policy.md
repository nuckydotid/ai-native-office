# Expenses policy

Owner: Ines Carvalho (founder). Last reviewed on 9 September 2026. All amounts are in US dollars (USD).

## What you can approve yourself

You can spend up to **USD 150 per purchase** on something the work needs, without asking anyone first. Keep the receipt. Anything above USD 150 needs Ines's approval in writing before you pay. A chat message in the lobby counts as writing.

Subscriptions and other recurring costs always need approval, whatever the amount, because they keep costing money after the first month.

## Receipts and payback

Send the receipt to Owen within **21 days** of the purchase. Approved expenses are paid back with the monthly payout on the last Friday of the month. A receipt that arrives late is paid in the next payout, and only if Ines agrees.

## Home workspace budget

New joiners get a one-off home workspace budget of **USD 400**, usable in their first 60 days, for a desk, chair, monitor or similar. It is separate from the self-approval limit above.

## Travel

- Fly economy class. Ines approves every trip before booking, including customer visits.
- Hotels: up to USD 160 per night, taxes included.
- Meals while travelling: up to USD 45 per day. Keep a receipt for each meal.
- Ground transport: public transport or a standard ride-hailing trip. No car rental without approval.

## Not covered

Personal items, alcohol, fines, and anything bought for a person outside the team.

## If you are unsure

Ask Owen before you buy. If you bought something by mistake, tell Owen. Honest mistakes are not a problem; hiding them is.
