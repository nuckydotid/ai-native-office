# FAQ for customers and support

Maintained by Owen Whitaker. These are the questions customers and new support staff ask most often.

## Which phones does the app run on?

The app runs on **iOS 16 or later** and on **Android 10 or later**. Tablets work too, in portrait and landscape.

## Which languages does the app speak?

English and Bahasa Indonesia. More languages are planned and will be announced in the release notes.

## Does the app work without a signal?

Jobs you have already opened stay available on the device, and a finished inspection that could not be uploaded waits on the phone and is sent when the connection returns. Creating and editing inspections without a signal is coming with offline sync; the roadmap says when.

## How many photos can I attach to an inspection?

Up to 40 photos per inspection. Photos are reduced to a size that keeps the inspection quick to upload.

## How do I add a customer signature?

Open the completion screen of the inspection and ask the customer to sign with a finger. The signature is saved as an image attached to the inspection.

## I forgot my password. What do I do?

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen. The reset email arrives within a few minutes. If it does not, check the spam folder and then contact support.

## How do I report a problem?

Open Help, then Send a report. The report includes the app version and the last few minutes of activity, never your photos. Support replies within one business day.

## How often does the app update?

New versions go out every second Tuesday through the app stores. A new version reaches users gradually over a few days, so you may get it later than a colleague.

## Who can see my inspections?

Your own team and your company's managers. Northwind staff see customer data only when support needs it to fix a problem, and only with the customer's permission.

## Who do I contact?

Support is available through the Help screen. For a pilot, your Northwind contact is Owen Whitaker.
