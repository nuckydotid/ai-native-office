# Customer onboarding playbook

Owner: Owen Whitaker (product). Last reviewed on 28 September 2026.

Every new customer starts with a pilot. A pilot is a small group of the customer's technicians using the app on real jobs, with us watching closely. The pilot is how we find out whether the app fits the customer's way of working before they roll it out to everyone.

## The steps

1. **Signature.** The customer signs the order form. Owen opens a customer workspace and invites the customer's named contact.
2. **Kickoff call.** Owen holds the kickoff call within **3 business days** of the signature. Agenda: who is in the pilot group, which checklists they use, which phones and tablets they have, and what success looks like.
3. **Set-up.** We load the customer's checklist templates and job types into their workspace. The customer sends a list of their technicians and the devices they use.
4. **Training.** One 60-minute video session for the pilot group, recorded for later joiners.
5. **Pilot.** The pilot group is **8 technicians**. The pilot runs for 28 days. Owen holds a check-in call every Thursday.
6. **Review.** At the end of the pilot we review the numbers together and decide how fast to roll out to the rest of the customer's technicians.
7. **Hand-over.** After the review the customer moves to the normal support route and the pilot group becomes the customer's own champions.

## What good looks like

- The first inspection is submitted by the customer within 7 business days of the kickoff call.
- At least 75% of the pilot group submit an inspection in each week of the pilot.
- No unresolved severity 1 or severity 2 incident touches the pilot group.

If the first week is quiet, Owen phones the customer's contact instead of waiting for the Thursday call.

## What we need from the customer

- A named contact who can make decisions.
- The list of pilot technicians and the devices they carry.
- Their current paper or spreadsheet checklists, so we can copy the wording exactly.
- Permission to use anonymised numbers (inspections per technician per day) in our own planning.
