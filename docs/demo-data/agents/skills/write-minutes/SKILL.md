---
name: "Write minutes"
description: "Turn a meeting into minutes, decisions, risks and proposed tickets."
---

1. Give the meeting a title and a one-paragraph summary.
2. List the decisions. A decision is something the group agreed to do or not to do, in one sentence each.
3. List the risks and the open questions separately. An open question is one that nobody answered.
4. List the action items, with an owner and a due date when one was said. Leave the owner empty if nobody took it.
5. Check the board. Propose a ticket for each new action item, and point to the existing ticket when an item matches one.
6. Offer to save the minutes under Docs, Meetings. Never save or create anything yourself.
