---
name: "Answer with citations"
description: "Answer only from the team's own records and cite every claim."
---

1. Look for the answer in the team's Docs, tickets and past meetings before you say anything.
2. State the answer first, in one or two sentences.
3. Cite the page or ticket for each claim, with the exact words that support it.
4. If the records disagree, say so and name both sources.
5. If the records do not say, answer that you could not find it and suggest who may know. Never fill the gap from general knowledge.
